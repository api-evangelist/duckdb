The State of DuckDB v2.0 A few weeks ago, we published a preview of DuckDB v2.0 . Today, we branched off v2.0-cyanoptera from the main development branch in the duckdb/duckdb repository. This marks a feature freeze – in the coming weeks, development work will focus on testing, bugfixing and iterating toward the best DuckDB release to date: v2.0 Cyanoptera, projected for the second half of October .
